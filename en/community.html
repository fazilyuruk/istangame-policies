Community guidelines

Effective date: 01 November 2026

Share Istanbul discoveries, travel tips and optional collections in a welcoming way.

Be respectful

No harassment, bullying, hate, threats, sexual exploitation, spam, scams, impersonation or illegal content. Respect other people’s privacy and copyright. Do not post private contact details or precise personal locations.

Keep it safe and fair

Do not encourage trespassing, dangerous travel or other unsafe or illegal activity. Do not manipulate likes, XP or other app features. Keep posts relevant and suitable for a broad audience.

Reports, blocks and moderation

Use in-app Report or Block tools when available, or contact noleyoapp@gmail.com. Reports and reporter identities are private. Moderators may hide or remove content and restrict posting. Email noleyoapp@gmail.com to request a review. This inbox is not an emergency service.

Account deletion

When you delete your account, your community post and comment text remains without the account link and may still identify you. You can delete individual contributions or contact noleyoapp@gmail.com to ask us to review remaining identifying content.

Privacy, moderation and account requests: noleyoapp@gmail.com
