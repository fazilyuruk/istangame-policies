Account deletion

Effective date: 01 November 2026

You can request deletion of your IstanGame account in the app or by email.

In the app

Open Profile → Account settings → Delete account and confirm. If the option is unavailable, email noleyoapp@gmail.com.

Without the app

Email noleyoapp@gmail.com with the subject “IstanGame account deletion”. Include your username or the email linked to your account so we can verify your request. Do not send your password or sign-in tokens.

What deletion does

Deletion removes private profile, Passport, trip, XP and reward information and the link between your account and your community posts. Your post and comment text remains without the account link and may still identify you. Delete individual posts or comments first, or contact us to ask about remaining identifying content. Minimal safety and deletion records may be retained.

Questions

We will contact you about your request or if further verification is needed. Contact noleyoapp@gmail.com.
