Privacy policy

Effective date: 01 November 2026

IstanGame is a city-exploration app for discovering Istanbul, recording experiences and sharing travel tips.

Operator and eligibility

IstanGame is operated by Fazıl Yürük, an individual based in Istanbul, Türkiye. Contact: noleyoapp@gmail.com.

You must be at least 13 to use IstanGame. Any higher age or parent/guardian consent requirement that applies where you live also applies.

Information and use

Google and Supabase Auth process sign-in details such as your email and account identity. IstanGame stores your username, display name, country, language and profile settings. It stores experience logs, dates, optional notes, XP, badges, trips and community activity you submit.

We use this information to run your account, show progress and community content, send optional notifications, answer requests and protect the service. We do not sell your personal information.

Location, maps and notifications

Location is optional. If you request it, the map uses a temporary foreground location to show your position. It is not sent to the IstanGame API, saved in your Passport or used to verify rewards. Map requests may reveal your IP address and displayed area to OpenFreeMap.

If you enable notifications, IstanGame uses a device token and related technical details to send alerts through Expo and Apple or Google push services. You can turn off location and notification permissions in your device settings.

Visibility and sharing

Leaderboard and public Passport visibility are on by default. You can change both in Profile. When public Passport is on, other people can see your completed experience names, including historical entries, and public profile details such as your username and progress. Passport notes, individual activity dates and trip details stay private.

Community posts, comments and collections can be seen by other users; some community pages are readable without signing in. Reports, blocks and moderator notes are private. Blocking limits signed-in interaction but cannot prevent people from viewing or copying public content. When you share or export information, the destination you choose receives that copy.

Storage, deletion and your choices

We keep account information while the account is active. We do not automatically delete inactive accounts. Use Profile → Account settings → Delete account, or email noleyoapp@gmail.com. Deletion removes private account information and removes the link between your account and community posts. Post and comment text remains and may still identify you. You can delete individual contributions or ask us to review identifying text. Some limited safety and deletion records may remain.

You can change visibility and device permissions, and request access, correction or deletion by contacting noleyoapp@gmail.com. Questions: noleyoapp@gmail.com. You can contact us with questions or requests about your information.
